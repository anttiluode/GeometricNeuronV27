A ring that remembers

Every dot below is the original 2025 Perception Lab loop with its Constant Signal set to 1.6, which makes it a silent relay. Each unit's two beat wires go to the next unit's setpoint_mod. Nothing else is added except that a wire can have a length. Tap a rhythm in and the ring keeps it.

1 · Ring memory

The strip is one lap of the ring as seen at unit 0: one cell per tick. Click a cell to write or erase that single bit. "Erase all" raises unit 0's setpoint for one lap. Try weight −5.5 (the hole), a little noise, or one unit with cable length 55 and the clock gating it (lap 59 matches the clock's 59 ticks).

2 · Distance picks the listener

One sender beats once. Forty-one receivers sit at cable lengths 0 to 40, and the signal weakens as exp(−length / length constant). A receiver beats only if what arrives lands in the narrow band a relay responds to, so a strong sender skips its near neighbours.

What was measured

QuestionResult
Does a ring keep a pattern?Rings of 4, 8 and 16 relays kept 40 of 40 random patterns of 4 bits per unit, unchanged for 1,000 laps (weight −6, no noise).
Is one unit enough?One relay with one cable back to itself kept 20 of 20 random patterns at every cable length tried, up to 256 slots.
Limit on the patternA run of 1s up to 48 ticks is kept exactly. Runs of 49 and 50 are not: the unit's 50-tick variance memory falls below 0.1 and it stops being a relay.
Erase (Argon's check)The 8-tick rhythm on signal_in at amplitudes 0.2 to 1.0 erased nothing in 12 of 12 rings. Raising one unit's setpoint by +12 for one lap erased everything; for one tick it removed exactly one bit, 12 of 12.
Weight bandExact from −4.9 to −6.55, with holes at −5.25 to −5.65 and −5.9. The hole is square size 0: a blank checkerboard sends nothing.
NoiseFragile. The wire signal is about 12 units. With noise of 0.05 all 8 rings survived 300 laps at weights −6.05 to −6.4; at 0.1 only at −6.15; at 0.2 at none.
Clock (Argon's check)A 59-tick clock gating a 32-tick ring empties it within 100 laps. Gating a 59-tick or 118-tick loop removes only a few slots and the rest stays for 600 laps.
Two-way runawayNot solved. A unit's own beat fed back on a 4-tick cable cuts a two-way chain from about 408 beats per unit to 1 or 2, at one weight and one length only.
